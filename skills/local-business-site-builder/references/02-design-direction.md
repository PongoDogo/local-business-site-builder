# 02 · Design direction (decided per client, never copied)

Verified: 2026-10-05. Contents: 1 Process · 2 Directions · 3 "AI look" tells · 4 Greek fonts · 5 Tokens and contrast · 6 Imagery · 7 Motion · 8 Worked example (do not copy) · 9 Logo

## 1. Process
1. Fill `docs/DESIGN.md` (generated by scaffold): essence, one reaction wanted, tone words, references, competitors to avoid.
2. Propose 2-3 named directions (§2) in 3 lines each (palette logic, type, motif, photography). The user picks ONE. No CSS before that.
3. Put tokens in `:root` (and `design.tokens` in the facts), declare `contrast_pairs`, pick the font (§4), define ONE motif that recurs subtly in 4-6 places (logo, hero, step connectors, dividers, map pin, loading states).
4. If a frontend-design/ui skill is available, use it for craft, but its font advice must be filtered through §4 (it often names fonts without Greek).
5. Never reuse the previous client's palette, motif or layout. The structure (sections, conversion mechanics) may repeat; the look must not.

## 2. Directions (starting points; adapt)
| Direction | Fits | Palette logic | Type | Motif / imagery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Emergency trade** | drains, locksmith, electrician, towing | deep ink + warm paper + one hot CTA colour (amber/orange); CTA is the loudest thing | sturdy sans (Commissioner, Roboto Slab+Fira Sans) | tool/flow motif from the trade; real van/work photos; hard-edge "pressable" buttons |
| **Clinical calm** | dentist, physio, clinics (check §Regulated) | cool white, one desaturated teal/blue, lots of air | humanist sans or serif+sans (EB Garamond + IBM Plex Sans) | thin lines, real room photos, no stock smiles |
| **Warm craft** | bakery, restaurant, florist, ceramics | earthy neutrals, one rich accent, paper textures sparingly | serif display + friendly sans (Brygada 1918 + Manrope) | food/product photography large; handwritten accents only as small details |
| **Industrial bold** | HVAC, roofing, construction, auto | near-black + safety yellow/orange, high contrast | condensed/heavy sans (Sofia Sans Condensed, Roboto Condensed) | diagonal cuts, numbered steps, hazard-stripe dividers used once |
| **Quiet premium** | lawyer, accountant, architect, boutique | monochrome + one metallic/ink accent | high-contrast serif (Source Serif 4/Literata) + Source Sans 3 | generous whitespace, no icons, text-led |
Each client gets a *named* direction in DESIGN.md (e.g. "Φούρνος: ζεστό αλεύρι"), not a category.

## 3. "AI look" tells to avoid (documented in Anthropic guidance, NN/g and agency write-ups)
Inter/Roboto/Arial as the only voice · indigo→purple gradients · three identical rounded cards with an icon · emoji as icons · same shadow on everything · everything centred · stock photos of smiling people (NN/g Aug 2026: users rate imagery lower once they suspect it is AI) · weightless copy ("ολοκληρωμένες λύσεις υψηλής ποιότητας") · decorative blobs. The audit flags emoji, filler phrases and em-dash overuse (COPY-xxx).

## 4. Greek fonts (hard rule)
- Only ~118 of 1,950 Google Fonts families (6.1%) include Greek. **No Greek:** Poppins, Montserrat, DM Sans, Lato, Raleway, Playfair Display, Space Grotesk, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Fraunces, Bricolage Grotesque, Nunito, Mulish, Merriweather, Rubik, Lexend, Cabin, Oswald. Greek text then silently falls back to a system font.
- **With Greek (mostly SIL OFL):** Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Fira Sans, Noto Sans/Serif, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans/Serif, Manrope, Commissioner (Greek designer), Sofia Sans (+Condensed), Geologica, Geom, Syne (display), Roboto Condensed, Roboto Slab (Apache 2.0), EB Garamond, Literata, Source Serif 4, Brygada 1918, Ubuntu/Ubuntu Sans (Ubuntu Font Licence).
- A Greek page needs BOTH the `greek` and `latin` subset files (digits, punctuation and € live in `latin`). Typical weight: 30-70 KB for both.
- Verify and self-host: `scripts/font_greek_check.mjs "Family" --download public/assets/fonts` (prints @font-face with unicode-range and preload tags). Keep the OFL text next to the files. Never link Google Fonts from the page (IP transfer without consent: LG München I, 3 O 17493/20, 20 Jan 2022; and it breaks the CSP).
- Check pairings visually in Greek (capitals with tonos in lowercase words, ΐ ΰ, ς). One family + one accent at most.

## 5. Tokens and contrast
Token names are fixed (the base CSS uses them): `--color-ink, -ink-deep, -text, -muted, -paper, -paper-alt, -surface, -line, -line-strong, -link, -call, -call-hover, -call-text, -on-dark, -on-dark-link, -focus, --font-sans`. WCAG AA: text 4.5:1, large text/UI 3:1 (`scripts/contrast.mjs`). Declare pairs in `design.contrast_pairs`; the audit fails below 4.5.

## 6. Imagery
Real photos only (van, work, premises, team). Alt text says what is really shown. WebP via `scripts/images.mjs` with real width/height and `srcset`/`sizes`; the first (hero) image `fetchpriority="high"`, never lazy. Before/after pairs need honest captions. No images of people the owner cannot show.

## 7. Motion
All animation inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. SMIL (`<animate>`, `<animateMotion>`) ignores media queries: hide the element by default (`display:none`) and show it inside the no-preference block. Intro animations run once, then calm. Scroll-driven effects (`animation-timeline: view()`) behind `@supports`. Never animate layout properties; no auto-playing video with sound; nothing that flashes.

## 8. Worked example (Τσαβάρης: understand, do NOT copy)
Concept: one metaphor (pipe/water) threaded through logo, mobile hero ring that "unblocks", desktop pipe drawing, step connectors with flowing water, star meter, map pulse. Palette: navy ink + warm paper + water blue + amber CTA with hard navy bottom shadow. Type: Commissioner variable, tabular numerals for phone/prices. Structure: top bar (status + phone) → hero (H1 service+city, XL call button, "από 40€" link, 4 trust points) → prices → reviews → before/after → services (folds) → why us → steps+van → areas → FAQ → big phone callout → footer. Lesson to reuse: *a single trade-specific idea carried subtly*, CTA as the loudest element, honest practical copy. Lesson NOT to reuse: colours, water motif, layouts, copy.

## 9. Logo
If the client has none: ask first; otherwise propose a simple wordmark/monogram as SVG (use a logo skill if available), test at 16 px and one-colour. `make_icons.mjs` builds favicon/icons from one SVG/PNG.
