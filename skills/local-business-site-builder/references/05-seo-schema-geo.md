# 05 · SEO, structured data, Google Business Profile, AI search

Verified: 2026-10-05 (Google docs summarised by tools: re-check exact wording before quoting). Contents: 1 What matters · 2 JSON-LD · 3 Removed/obsolete · 4 Technical SEO · 5 Google Business Profile · 6 AI search and llms.txt · 7 Greek market · 8 Area pages

## 1. What matters
Relevance + Business Profile + reviews + on-page clarity + consistent NAP (name, address, phone). Whitespark 2026 expert survey weights: GBP signals ~32%, reviews ~20% (secondary). Google is ~95% of Greek search (Statcounter Sep 2026), Bing ~3%.

## 2. JSON-LD (generated by `scaffold.mjs derive` from the facts)
- One `@graph`: LocalBusiness **subtype** (Plumber, Electrician, Dentist…: most specific; array for several; `additionalType` unsupported) + WebPage + WebSite linked by `@id`.
- Google (LocalBusiness doc, updated 2026-09-08): required `name`, `address`; recommended `geo` (≥5 decimals), `openingHoursSpecification`, `telephone`, `url`, `priceRange` (<100 chars). 24 h = `00:00`–`23:59`; closed all day = `00:00`–`00:00`.
- Also useful (schema.org, not in Google's doc): `areaServed`, `hasMap`, `sameAs`, `taxID`, `legalName`, `hasOfferCatalog`. There is no ΓΕΜΗ property.
- `sameAs`: only real, populated, owned profiles (Maps CID, Facebook page). Never the client's other empty domain.
- Prices only if visible; omit `priceRange` unless exact. No `aggregateRating`/`review` on the business itself.
- `image` files must exist (absolute URLs); `telephone` E.164; `url` = canonical.

## 3. Removed/obsolete (do not add for SEO gain)
FAQ rich results retired 7 May 2026 (Search Console/Rich Results Test support ended June-Aug 2026). Also gone/deprecated: HowTo, sitelinks search box, special announcement, course info, estimated salary, learning video, vehicle listing, practice problem. Still in the gallery: local business, organization, review snippet, breadcrumb, video, event, product. FAQPage markup is harmless but useless.

## 4. Technical SEO rules
- One canonical host (apex + https). Signals by strength: 301/308 > `rel=canonical` > sitemap; 302/307 are weak. Google follows ≤10 redirect hops. Every page: self-referencing absolute canonical = sitemap URL.
- robots.txt: `User-agent: *` / `Allow: /` / `Sitemap: https://DOMAIN/sitemap.xml`. Never leave a staging `Disallow: /` (audit SEO-001). `noindex` does not work inside robots.txt; a noindex page must be crawlable.
- sitemap: only indexable, 200, canonical URLs; `lastmod` only if truthfully maintained (Google ignores `priority`/`changefreq`).
- 404 must return 404 (not 200 soft-404) with a noindex page; 404 and 410 are treated alike.
- Hosting preview domain (`*.hostingersite.com`): Hostinger serves a platform robots.txt blocking only Googlebot and documents nothing about indexing → 301 to the canonical host and remove the preview domain in hPanel.
- Titles ≤ ~60 chars, unique; descriptions 90-160 chars with phone. Greek-only site: no hreflang needed (if added: `el`, self-referencing, reciprocal, `x-default`).
- OG image 1200×630 PNG with brand + headline + phone; icons incl. maskable; theme-color = manifest.
- Search Console: add property, submit sitemap, request indexing of `/`.

## 5. Google Business Profile (the owner's, not the site's)
Name = real-world name (no keywords/phone/hours). Service-area businesses hide the address when no customers visit; up to 20 areas (~2 h drive). NAP must equal the site. Reviews: never gate or incentivise; staff review quotas and soliciting by staff name were added to the prohibited list in April 2026 (secondary). Review link: GBP → "Get more reviews" (official) or `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=PLACE_ID` (widely used, not documented by Google; audit REV-010 flags links that open the listing instead).

## 6. AI search and llms.txt
Google (15 May 2026 guide; Mueller 2 Jun 2026) says Search does not use llms.txt and needs no special file. Ahrefs (137k domains, Jun 2026): 28% publish it, 97% of those saw zero requests in May 2026; SE Ranking (~300k): no link to AI citations. No OpenAI/Anthropic statement found. → `llms.txt` is optional, cheap, harmless; the scaffold generates it (`features.llms_txt:false` disables). Do not sell it as SEO.
Crawlers: OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot = search (blocking removes you from those answers); GPTBot, ClaudeBot = training; ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User = user-triggered fetches (may ignore robots.txt); Google-Extended does not affect Search. Default for a local business: allow all. Content that gets cited: clear facts, structured pages, honest FAQs (evidence is weak: Princeton GEO paper only).

## 7. Greek market
ΑΦΜ/ΓΕΜΗ in the footer are trust and legal signals (see 06). Directories (Χρυσός Οδηγός, Vrisko) matter only per agency blogs: keep NAP identical if listed. No authoritative source on Greeklish/diacritic handling: write natural Greek with accents.

## 8. Area pages
Templated "plumber in {town}" pages are doorway/scaled-content risk (Google spam policies). Single page with region lists is the default; add an area page only with unique content (real jobs, local specifics).
